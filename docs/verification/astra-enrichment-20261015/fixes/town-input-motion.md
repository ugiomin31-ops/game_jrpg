## Limits

The corner repair is bounded collision-aware pursuit, not pathfinding: at most three follower moves per frame for the existing four-member party, no growing trail or search queue. Followers can lag or wait behind an obstacle until their direct pursuit route opens; they never intentionally teleport to catch up. This conservative behavior needs the runtime checks above, particularly around long barriers. Existing spawn/roster placement and authored collider accuracy remain unchanged; report 35 finding 1 (tree collision asset placement) is outside this slice. No runtime, visual, compilation or test-pass claim is made.
